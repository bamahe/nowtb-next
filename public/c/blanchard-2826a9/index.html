About these numbers

  • Monthly estimates include principal and interest, mortgage insurance, HOA, any CDD, and property taxes at about 1.45% of price. They do not include homeowners insurance. FHA figures assume 3.5% down.
  • New homes show $0 tax today because only the land was taxed last year. Plan on the full tax bill once the home is assessed.
  • The 4.99% and 5.49% rates came from the sales team on tour. Highland's published Fall Sale terms describe a 2.5% buydown concession through its lender partners, so we'll confirm the exact rate per home in writing.
  • Rates, prices and incentives change often. Nothing here is a loan approval or a rate lock.
