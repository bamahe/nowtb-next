The short versionWhere the money and the rules line up

The best nightly-STR buys right now are in unincorporated Pinellas County: Seminole, Largo and Palm Harbor addresses that sit outside city limits. Nightly rentals are legal there with a county Certificate of Use, the homes are 1 to 4 miles from the Gulf beaches, and they out-earn Tampa homes with the same bedroom count.

Inside Tampa city limits, residential zoning requires stays of 7 nights or more. True nightly Airbnb in Tampa only works on commercial, mixed-use or downtown zoned property. I pulled every one of those from the MLS. The best Tampa-side option is Temple Terrace, the city next door, where nightly stays are legal in regular pool-home neighborhoods. Both sections are below.

Straight talk on cash flow. At market-average bookings, none of these pay for themselves with 25% down at today's STR loan rates. Breaking even takes roughly 40% to 50% down, an all-cash purchase, or top-quartile revenue. The real return on these homes is the first-year tax deduction from cost segregation, strong long-term appreciation near the Gulf, and personal use. If he wants monthly cash flow first, we adjust the target to cash purchases or larger down payments.

How I ran it

  • Gross: midpoint of the range shown for each home, based on area averages for that bedroom count. Cleaning fees paid by guests are left out on both sides.
  • Expenses: platform fees 3%, repairs and reserves 6%, utilities and internet $6,000 to $9,000, supplies about $2,000, pool service $1,800 to $2,400, lawn and pest $1,500, STR insurance $5,500 to $8,000, licenses $500 to $1,200, property taxes at about 1.55% of price (Pinellas, non-homestead).
  • Managed: adds a 20% professional management fee. Self-run: no management fee. Self-running also matters for the tax strategy below.
  • Loan: 75% loan, 30-year fixed at 7.25%. DSCR loans were near 6.75% for long-term rentals in early October 2026, and STR loans usually price higher. Cash to close adds 3% closing costs and furnishing where needed.
  • Cost seg estimate: assumes 80% of price is building and about 27% of that qualifies for first-year bonus depreciation. Illustration only; his CPA and a cost seg engineer set the real figure.

NeighborhoodsWhere these homes sit

Seminole, unincorporated

Picks 1, 2, 4 and 6 · 263 active STRs in the Seminole market

Established 1960s to 1990s block homes on large lots with no HOAs, between Lake Seminole and the Intracoastal. Indian Rocks and Indian Shores beaches are a short drive over the Walsingham and Park Blvd bridges. Lake Seminole Park, the Pinellas Trail and Seminole City Center cover parks, biking and shopping. Families and snowbirds book here for longer winter stays. Pools are standard: about 71% of local STRs have one.

West Largo, near Indian Rocks

Pick 5 · 457 active STRs in the Largo market

Quiet streets west of Seminole Blvd, minutes from the Indian Rocks Beach causeway and Walsingham Park. The draw is beach access without beach prices or beach flood zones. Largo has the deepest STR supply in the area, so a well-reviewed, well-priced listing matters more here.

Palm Harbor

Pick 3 · North Pinellas

A calmer, family market in north Pinellas near Honeymoon and Caladesi Islands, Ozona's waterfront dining and downtown Dunedin. Innisbrook hosts the PGA Tour's Valspar Championship each March, which lifts spring demand. Large homes with space for two families do well here.

Tampa proper

Tampa options T1 to T6 · strong demand, tough rules

Tampa has the biggest event calendar (Lightning, Bucs, Gasparilla, conventions, cruises), but homes in residential zoning must rent for 7 nights or more. That cuts nightly income and puts the cost seg strategy at risk. The Tampa options in this brief are all commercially or downtown zoned, which is what makes short stays legal there.

For his CPA: the 7-day test. Using cost seg depreciation against other income generally depends on an average guest stay of 7 days or less, plus his own material participation in running the rental. The nightly-legal Pinellas homes clear the stay test easily; Tampa's 7-night floor sits right on the line. Hiring a full-service manager can make material participation harder to prove. His CPA should confirm the plan before he picks a home.
