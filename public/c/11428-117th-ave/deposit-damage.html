Two different clocks, and they are short

Florida runs two deadlines off the day the tenant vacated, and which one applies depends on what you decide to do.

15 days If you are not claiming any of it. The full deposit has to be returned within fifteen days of the tenant vacating. No notice required, just return it.
30 days If you are claiming any of it. Written notice of the claim has to go out by certified mail within thirty days of the tenant vacating, and it has to state what you are claiming and why. That is what the first table on this page is for.
then 15 Once that notice lands, the tenant has fifteen days to object in writing. If he does not, you deduct the claim and send the balance.

Miss the thirty day notice and the claim is gone, regardless of how good the documentation is. That is why this page exists and why it is dated.
