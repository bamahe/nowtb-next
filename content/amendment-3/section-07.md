# ## How Do You Vote on Amendment 3?

Amendment 3 is **Ballot Number 3** on Florida's November 3, 2026 general election ballot. On the ballot it reads "Increased Homestead Exemption; Lower Cap on Increases in Non-Homestead Property Assessments." There is no financial impact statement printed under it, because legislatively referred amendments are not required to carry one.

It passes only with **at least 60% approval** from voters. Anything under 60% fails. That threshold is why a measure can win a clear majority and still not become law.

### Key dates for the 2026 general election

| What | When |
|---|---|
| Voter registration deadline | October 5, 2026 (closed) |
| Deadline to request a mailed ballot | 5:00 p.m. on October 22, 2026 |

| Election Day | November 3, 2026, polls open 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. local time |

Registration for this election closed on October 5. If you missed it, register for future elections at [RegistertoVoteFlorida.gov](https://registertovoteflorida.gov/).

### Three ways to cast your vote

Request a mailed ballot, vote during the early voting window, or vote at your precinct on Election Day. For early voting and Election Day you must bring a **current and valid photo ID with a signature**. If your photo ID has no signature, bring a second ID that does.

A mailed ballot must be in your county Supervisor of Elections office by 7:00 p.m. on Election Day. Postmarks do not count. Dropping it off beats mailing it late.

### Check your status at the official sources

- **Registration status:** [registration.dos.fl.gov/CheckVoterStatus](https://registration.dos.fl.gov/CheckVoterStatus). Enter your name and date of birth.
- **Register or update your record:** [RegistertoVoteFlorida.gov](https://registertovoteflorida.gov/)
- **Polling place, sample ballot, and vote-by-mail ballot tracking:** your county Supervisor of Elections. Find yours through the [Division of Elections directory](https://dos.fl.gov/elections/contacts/supervisor-of-elections/).
- **Official amendment information:** [Florida Division of Elections, Constitutional Amendments](https://dos.fl.gov/elections/laws-rules/constitutional-amendments/)

Your county sample ballot shows Amendment 3 exactly as it appears on November 3. I tell every client to pull theirs and read the amendment language before heading to the polls.
