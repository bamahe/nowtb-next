Your Buyer

How we get you
to full price.

At $264,900, a gated townhome with an attached garage is exactly what first time buyers, veterans and local workforce buyers are searching for. These buyers are well qualified, but most of them are short on cash for the down payment and closing costs, not on income. Solve the cash and they pay your price instead of negotiating it down.

That is where most listings leave money on the table. I work closely with lenders every week, so I know the current rates and the programs that are open right now. When a buyer's agent calls, I make sure their buyer is using every program they qualify for, and I coach that agent on how to write an offer at our number.

Hometown Heroes
Florida's workforce program
Down payment and closing cost help for teachers, nurses, first responders, military and other Florida workers. Brandon is full of these buyers.
VA
Zero down for veterans
MacDill is close by. A VA buyer needs no down payment, which leaves room to offer at full price.
DPA
Down payment assistance
State and Hillsborough County assistance programs for first time buyers, often stacked with FHA financing.

Credit beats a price cut

When a buyer needs help, they usually ask for a lower price. A closing cost credit almost always does more for them and costs you the same or less. A $5,000 price reduction saves a buyer roughly $30 to $35 a month on their payment. A $5,000 credit covers cash they do not have at the closing table. Structured this way, your sale price holds, and the appraisal backs it up because the comps support $264,900.
