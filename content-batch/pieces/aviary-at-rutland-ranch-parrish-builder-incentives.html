What I would check before signing

  1. Get an outside Loan Estimate. Every offer here is tied to the builder's lender. A big closing cost credit can be offset by a higher rate or fees. Compare the total cost over the years you expect to own the home, not just the headline number.
  2. Understand the ARM before you take it. 4.25% for five years is real savings up front. The rate can then rise up to 1% a year. It fits a buyer who expects to move or refinance inside about seven years. It is a poor fit if a higher payment later would strain your budget.
  3. Ask for the CDD amount on the exact homesite. HOA dues are $400 per quarter and include irrigation water. The CDD is on top of that and is part of your monthly cost.
  4. Ask if the price reduction is already in the tag. The builder notes some homes are advertised with the reduction already applied.
  5. Still get inspections. New does not mean flawless. A third party inspection before closing is cheap protection.
  6. Verify schools for your address. The community lists Annie Lucy Williams Elementary, Buffalo Creek Middle and Parrish Community High in the School District of Manatee County. Confirm the assignment for the specific home.

About the community

Amenities include a heated pool and pool house, a playground, walking trails, two pickleball courts and a half basketball court, with lakes, ponds and wetlands conservation through the neighborhood. Bradenton, Lakewood Ranch, Sarasota and St. Petersburg are all a reasonable drive, and I-75 is close for commuters.
